Privacy Policy

BBAN Sudan | Effective Date: November 3, 2026

BBAN Sudan (بيبان السودان) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the application handles your information.

1. Information Collection

The application does not require registration or login. It does not collect personal information or transmit users' banking account numbers to external servers.

2. Banking Information

BBAN Sudan converts standard Sudanese bank account numbers into the BBAN format. Conversion is performed locally on your device. Account numbers entered for conversion are not sent to external servers.

Never enter your banking password or PIN into this application.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' personal information with third parties.

4. Advertising and Tracking

The application does not display advertisements or use analytics or tracking services to monitor users.

5. Data Security

Conversion takes place locally on your device. We do not operate external servers to collect banking account numbers through the conversion feature.

6. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page.
